CLUB EMERALD KNOWLEDGE BASE (edit this file; restart the agent after changes)
Address: Club Emerald, next to Sushrut & Mangal Anand Hospital, Swastik Park, off Eastern Express Highway, Chembur East, Mumbai 400071. Search "Club Emerald, Chembur" on Google Maps.
About: urban sports and lifestyle club with a boutique hotel, spa, banquets. 100% PURE VEGETARIAN everywhere (no meat, fish, egg). Jain and vegan options on request. No golf course. No pets allowed anywhere. Smoking: rooms are smoke-free, there is a designated smoking area.

MEMBERSHIP
- Starts from Rs 3 lakh; exact price depends on category (Family, Single, Senior Citizen, Corporate), tenure and members. Sales manager gives the quote on a tour. Always offer a tour or callback.
- Tenures: only 15 or 20 years (corporate: 15 years). No life membership, no zero-AMC plan. Annual Maintenance Charge (AMC) is billed yearly; no freezing/pausing; unpaid AMC suspends privileges with late fees.
- Family plan: husband, wife and up to 2 children under 18. Unmarried daughters over 18 stay covered until marriage. Sons leave the family plan at 18, married daughters leave too; they can take an individual membership or enter as accompanied guests (daily guest fee).
- Memberships are generally non-transferable; upgrade from individual to family is possible at the administration office.
- Corporate: 2 executive nominations per package; buy more packages in blocks of two; 10+ executives get a custom tier from the management committee.
- Senior citizens (60+): quiet reading lounges, wellness programs, cultural evenings.
- No in-house EMI or instalments; full payment upfront. Members can use their own credit card/bank EMI.
- Members get 15-25% off direct room rates, spa discounts, 15% off food and alcohol at Adda, priority room booking in peak seasons, discounted rates for private events.

GUESTS AND DRESS CODE
- Members can bring guests for a standard guest fee (depends on membership type); no fixed limit, big groups need arrangement.
- Non-members: no day passes for gym or pool. They can stay at the hotel (free pool, gym, sports access), or enter as a member's accompanied guest for a daily fee. Dining venues are for members and hotel guests; non-members can order via Zomato and Swiggy.
- General dress: smart casual. Banquets: formal or festive traditional wear. Avoid sportswear, flip-flops, swimwear outside pool areas.
- Gym dress: t-shirt/tank, track pants/shorts/tights, clean non-marking sports shoes. No jeans, trousers, kurtas, sarees, sandals, slippers, bare feet, heavy jewellery or loose chains.

AGE RULES
- Badminton and squash: under 18 need adult supervision or coaching (non-marking shoes mandatory). A 10-year-old can play with an adult.
- Gym and pool: ages 8 to 17 with adult or trainer supervision. Under 8 not allowed in gym, pool, hot tub or health club.

SPORTS AND FACILITIES
- The Courts: indoor badminton, wooden flooring. The Cube: private air-conditioned squash courts. Spinz: table tennis. Green Room: billiards and snooker. Card Room: bridge, rummy. Carrom and chess lounges.
- Ripples pool (lobby level): half-Olympic size, mornings 7 AM-12 PM and evenings 4-9 PM. No adult-only hours. Quiet times: 7-9 AM and 8-9 PM; busiest weekday 5-7 PM. Swimwear rental not available (bring your own); flotation aids provided. Changing rooms, showers, steam room.
- Swimming coaching: group Rs 4,500-6,500 per 12-session batch (weekday evenings 4-7 PM); personal Rs 8,000-12,000+ per batch; age 8+; members get discounts.
- Burn Gym (level two): cardio, strength, certified trainers, free lockers and towels, showers, steam. Hours: Mon-Sat 7 AM-10 PM, Sunday 9 AM-4 PM.
- Kids: Mischief outdoor play area near the pool; Papyrus Library on the lobby level.
- Spa, The White Lotus Spa: open to everyone, daily 11 AM-8 PM, book in advance via front desk. Five therapy rooms, a couple's suite. Express therapies Rs 1,199-1,800+, full-body 60-90 min Rs 2,200-3,200+, specialised (Thai, Balinese) Rs 3,000-4,500+. Members and hotel guests get discounts.

DINING
- Paprika: flagship multi-cuisine (North Indian, Italian, Pan-Asian, Continental). Patio: open-air poolside, South Indian, coffee, casual. The Nook: bakery, 100% eggless cakes, pastries, chocolates (lobby level).
- Adda, The Liquid Lounge (first floor): full bar with spirits, cocktails, wines, beer; vegetarian snacks. Mon-Fri 7 AM-11:45 PM; weekends 12-3 PM and 7-11:45 PM.

HOTEL ROOMS
- Superior (about 16 sq m / 193 sq ft, up to 2 guests): Rs 5,000-6,200 per night. Deluxe (about 26 sq m, up to 3 guests, king bed): Rs 6,500-7,800. Suite (about 35 sq m, up to 4 guests, separate living area): Rs 8,500-11,000+. Rates are dynamic; GST up to 18% extra. Non-members from about Rs 7,000+ depending on dates.
- Included: buffet breakfast, free Wi-Fi, free self-parking, in-room safe, pool, gym and sports access, 24-hour security.
- Check-in 2 PM, check-out 12 noon. Early check-in or late check-out subject to availability; before 9 AM arrival or after 2 PM departure usually costs a half or full day.
- Children under 7 stay free with existing bedding (breakfast may be charged). Age 7+ counts as adult and needs an extra rollaway bed (max 1 per room). No baby cots or cribs.
- 24-hour room service, pure vegetarian; full menu until 11 PM, then a late-night menu. Jain meals on request.
- Direct booking: free sports access, parking, spa discounts, best for long stays; online travel sites sometimes have deeper promotional discounts.
- Pets: not allowed.

BANQUETS AND EVENTS
- Venues: Royale, Imperial, Lawn; from 25 to 1,250 guests. Per-plate pure veg buffet starting around Rs 1,500 per plate, includes starters, mains, desserts, beverages, AC, table setup. Price varies with guest count, date (weekend evenings cost more) and customisation (live counters, Jain menus). 18% GST extra on food and beverage.
- In-house catering only; no outside caterers or non-veg ever.
- Decor: in-house empanelled decor team (professional decor from about Rs 50,000 per area). Outside decorators: policy is being confirmed, say the banquet team will confirm.
- DJ: in-house DJ available; outside DJ has a royalty fee from about Rs 15,000. Amplified music must end by 10-11 PM.
- Alcohol: allowed. In-house liquor packages (no corkage), or outside liquor with management approval plus corkage and a one-day FL-4 excise licence (team can help). Service must end by 11:30 PM-12 AM.
- Parking: free for banquet guests; valet for weddings/functions arranged via organiser; main lot holds 50-100 cars.
- Booking ahead: weddings (Nov-Mar) 8-12 months; social functions 3-6 months; corporate 1-3 months.
- Deposit: 30% standard, 50% for all-inclusive packages. Banquet bookings are non-refundable and non-cancellable. Balance before the event.
